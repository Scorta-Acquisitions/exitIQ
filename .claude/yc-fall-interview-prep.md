Part I — The Three Answers That Anchor the Interview
1. What changed since May?
“Last time, you said there might be a structural reason our clients were not getting bought. There was. We were doing the seller prep well, then restarting the hardest part—the buyer search—from zero on every deal. So we flipped the order. We built the capital-verified buyer pool first and rebuilt the agents around speed-to-close. Since May, two clients have been bought, we have collected $110K in success fees, $173K in total revenue, and one more deal is under contract at $1.7M.”


2. What is Scorta?
“Scorta is replacing the small business broker. We work with owners of $500K to $2M businesses and run the sale end to end with two teams of AI agents. The first team turns a messy owner-operated business into a financeable, sellable asset—recasting the financials, mapping owner dependency, and packaging the SBA pre-qualification. The second team matches it to our pool of capital-verified buyers and runs the deal room, diligence, lender process, and close. We grow that buyer pool through DealIQ, which lets searchers and acquisition entrepreneurs use the same recast engine to evaluate deals. Owners pay us $3K to $5K upfront and 5% when the business sells.”


3. What is the clearest proof it works?
“Our first closed client had already failed with a traditional broker. It sat on the market at $950K and did not sell. We signed it April 17, rebuilt the financials and lender package, matched it through our buyer pool, signed an LOI four weeks later at $1.3M, and closed 30 days after that. The same business, the same market, a different process—and a 36.8% higher price.”


The simple story underneath every answer
The seller side creates differentiated, financeable inventory.
The buyer side aggregates the scarce demand before a listing starts.
The agent stack removes most of the labor while humans retain judgment and approval.
The business earns $50K–$70K per close, not a small SaaS subscription.
The two closes prove possibility. The next challenge is proving repeatability.
