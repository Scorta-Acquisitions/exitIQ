# 8_decision_ready_white_space_and_diligence_priorities

The most defensible white space is a **sale-readiness operating layer for sub-USD 2M businesses** that does four things together: recasts financials deterministically, measures owner dependency and concentration risk, creates lender-ready evidence, and maintains a continuously updated sellability score. Scorta is closest in public thesis because its readiness dimensions, 90-day fix list, SBA pre-screen and staged lender-review package are explicit. [8_decision_ready_white_space_and_diligence_priorities[0]] [8][8_decision_ready_white_space_and_diligence_priorities[1]] [9] But no public evidence proves its internal ingestion stack or lender-portal work. The opportunity is therefore not to repeat the claim; it is to prove the handoff.

Eilla owns the strongest **high-value sell-side execution** position. The CreateX/Native Digital transaction and its 15-day offer claim show that its data-led buyer search can be attached to an actual advisory process. [8_decision_ready_white_space_and_diligence_priorities[2]] [3][8_decision_ready_white_space_and_diligence_priorities[3]] [2] Its challenge is economic and segmental: a roughly 5% fee on EUR 10M to EUR 20M deals does not automatically translate into an efficient sub-USD 2M model. [8_decision_ready_white_space_and_diligence_priorities[3]] [2] A competitor could attack below Eilla by productizing the preparation that Eilla currently performs inside an advisory mandate.

Bizbe owns the strongest **trust and transaction-room UX** in public copy. Its proof-of-funds gate, KYC, private listings and live room make the buyer journey concrete. [8_decision_ready_white_space_and_diligence_priorities[4]] [5] It also has the clearest current seller price, at 6.99%, once the onboarding package is included. [8_decision_ready_white_space_and_diligence_priorities[5]] [20] Its open questions are operating rather than conceptual: how many of the six visible opportunities close, whether the FedEx specialization produces repeatable sourcing, and whether home-services inventory is material. [8_decision_ready_white_space_and_diligence_priorities[6]] [16]

For a competitor or investor, the next evidence request should be narrowly operational:

1. **Eilla**: request a dated mandate table showing deal size, sector, geography, signed NDA count, offers, close status and fee for every mandate after April 2026; request the sub-EUR/USD 5M fee card and one US or home-services reference.
2. **Bizbe**: request the legal entity's state licensing matrix, anonymized closed-deal count, seller onboarding invoices, the six-listing category mix, proof for the USD 10M-plus FedEx claim and the exact conditions for buyer representation fees.
3. **Scorta**: request a live lender handoff, a named SBA lender or written lender workflow, anonymized pre/post readiness scores, a customer-approved closing reference, and evidence that QBO/Gusto/Stripe/POS ingestion and continuous rescore are deployed rather than planned.

These requests test the mechanisms that matter. They do not reward generic AI claims, database size or polished demos unless those assets change time-to-close, lender approval, buyer conversion or realized fees.
