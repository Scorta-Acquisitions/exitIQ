### Research executive summary

Eilla is a **mandate-led advisory**; Bizbe is a **broker-platform hybrid**. They are not interchangeable software products.

Eilla’s public stack is deeper: 8M+ company screening, buyer-thesis data, CIM/model automation, AI-assisted diligence. Bizbe’s public stack is a clearer owner workflow: valuation, listing, KYC, deal room. The open slot is connecting Eilla-like agent depth to Bizbe-like transaction UX — plus the financing/closing control plane neither shows.

Eilla’s strongest proof is a named deal: CreateX and Native Digital → White Pearl Technology Group, reported by [Tech.eu on 8 Apr 2026](https://tech.eu/2026/04/08/eilla-ai-executes-europes-first-ai-native-ma-deal/). Bizbe’s $10M+ FedEx ISP volume was not independently corroborated. Bizbe’s [disclosures](https://www.bizbe.com/disclosures) (effective 26 Jun 2026) now state **$2,500 seller onboarding, 6.99% success, $1,500/mo buy-side**, while the [homepage](https://www.bizbe.com/) still says free-to-start and pay-on-close. Neither has public, independently verified HVAC/plumbing closes. Neither shows lender APIs, escrow, longitudinal keep-sellable monitoring, or an auditable agent control plane.

## 2. What each is doing right

**Eilla**
1. **Institutional process at a size boutiques skip** — CIM, model, and a diligence workspace with named senior review. That is the real answer to “fees can’t carry a real team,” not the AI slogan. ([Technology](https://eilla.ai/our-technology))
2. **Buyer discovery as a product** — 8M+ screen, licensed data, buyer-supplied theses, ranked fit with a written rationale. CreateX/Native is evidence this can surface a non-obvious public acquirer.
3. **Competitive-process design** — published funnel aims at five offers, not a bilateral handshake. That is how they argue for price.
4. **One named close plus a PR machine** — Tech.eu, homepage logo row. One real transaction beats ten “AI-native” claims.
5. **Content that manufactures category** — SaaS multiples, PE criteria, European succession. Matches their CCO’s motion: sellers need to see the tech before they convert.
6. **Capital-efficient seed** — $1.5M, ~10 people, talking like a bank. If the Apr 2026 success-fee math is still true, one close funds a lot of AI. The current site no longer lets you verify that math.

**Bizbe**
1. **Owner-plain language** — vetted buyers, live room, pay on close. A Main Street owner understands this in one screen. ([Homepage](https://www.bizbe.com/))
2. **FedEx ISP beachhead with live inventory** — asking prices, stages, cash-flow figures. Niche density beats a generalist “we also do home services.”
3. **Workflow, not classifieds** — valuation → private list → KYC/POF → deal room → close support. “A broker, not a marketplace” is the right frame even if the legal docs narrow it.
4. **June disclosure made economics inspectable** — $2,500 + 6.99% + $1,500/mo. More honest than Eilla’s current site, even with the homepage contradiction. ([Disclosures](https://www.bizbe.com/disclosures))
5. **Distribution where sellers already look** — [BizBuySell broker profile](https://www.bizbuysell.com/business-broker/stephen-mckinney/bizbe/43975) plus ISP/SBA SEO.
6. **Founder-as-seller story** — repeat operators, not ex-Citi. Trust signal Eilla cannot copy without sounding like a bank.

**Scorta (already true in the docs, not yet market-proven)**
1. **The unclaimed question** — is this sellable, and if not, what would make it sellable? Eilla starts at mandate. Bizbe starts at listing. Continuity Evidence starts before either.
2. **Lender-shaped math** — SOP 50 10 8.1 (1.25× DSCR, $3M QoE line), DealIQ, four-state bridge with unsupported cash = zero. Neither competitor publishes this.
3. **Agent depth with named human gates and provenance** — beats Eilla’s copilot screenshots and Bizbe’s undisclosed models *if* you run it on live files.
4. **Vertical constraint** — HVAC/plumbing/electrical with refused categories. Bizbe’s home-services claim is still a nav label.
5. **Honesty as product** — don’t-list, non-exclusive activation, owner-controlled data. Opposite of Eilla’s old “25% higher valuations” (now off-site) and Bizbe’s “we don’t verify the numbers.”
6. **Rails ambition neither has language for** — prep now, wholesale or own-process later, take-rate on the transaction. Stripe-for-M&A is a different company than an AI-augmented broker.

## 3. Differentiation map

**Where Scorta already has (or can credibly claim) a superior angle vs both**

Pre-trigger sale-readiness: books connected, source-proven earnings, owner-authority drill, one verified fix — before anyone is shopping a CIM. Lender-ready packaging under the $3M QoE threshold rather than “AI valuation.” Home-services operating reality (dispatch, techs, memberships, owner-on-the-truck) instead of agency/SaaS comps or FedEx settlement statements. Auditable agent handoffs with a named human on every external artifact. Outcome-neutral rails: family, employee, outside broker, or Scorta — owner keeps the file.

**Where they look stronger — and what that implies**

- **Eilla wins pedigree, PR, buyer graph, CIM/outreach.** Do not try to out-Jefferies them on a €15M digital-agency auction. Steal ranked buyers and competitive tension *after* a package is financeable. Your demo cannot be a CIM factory; it has to be connected books → one-page financeable / not yet / never-on-7(a), in a founder-day.
- **Bizbe wins owner UX, live listings, deal-room theater, BizBuySell.** Listed-owner capture is their home field. Do not become a prettier BizBuySell. Do not open a logistics-route front. Stay in the trade.
- **Both look like a place a seller can start this week.** Continuity’s $1,500 paid start is slower at the front door. That is acceptable only if CPA/P&C actually delivers owners. If it does not, you are in their fight without their inventory.

**White space**

| Lane | Open for Scorta |
|---|---|
| Agent depth | Control plane: task, confidence, source, human decision, SLA. Case Manager as the product |
| Lender integration | Standard export. Blinded missing-item test. Three unaffiliated lenders. Never say bankable |
| Sale-readiness | Keep-sellable / Continuity Evidence. 30-day delta. Permanently financeable state |
| Home services | Recurring service revenue, tech retention, fleet, memberships, warranty, owner-on-truck replacement cost |
| Transparency | Provenance timeline — who touched what, which add-back is supported, which buyer saw which file, fee accrued — not a ticker that disclaims the numbers |
| Transaction rails | Long arc: consents, lender conditions, closing-agent handoff, post-close transition. **Not this quarter’s build** |

## 4. How Scorta offers a meaningfully better service

**Product / workflow**
- Double down: owner-initiated QuickBooks plus one independent source; four-state earnings bridge; authority drill; one verified remediation with before/after. That is the service neither competitor sells.
- Build next, only after three paid cases hit the same friction: analyst workbench, then connectors actually observed, then one frozen lender export. Reuse DealIQ on seller books. Do not start a second engine.
- Do not build: public listings, CIM factory, ranked buyer passport, SBA badge, valuation theater. Those are how you become a worse Eilla or a slower Bizbe.

**Positioning**

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| AI-native broker / AI-augmented advisory (Eilla’s category) | We keep the business runnable and the earnings source-proven before anyone shops it |
| Transparent marketplace / live deal room (Bizbe’s category) | You can see the evidence lineage. We do not list numbers we have not supported |
| Higher valuation / faster close as the lead promise | Financeable as-is, financeable if restructured, or honest never-on-7(a) |
| We run your entire sale (Bizbe headline their own disclosure walks back) | Continuity now. Licensed transaction later, optional, non-exclusive. You keep the file |
| Bankable / pre-qualified / guaranteed SBA | Pre-underwriting inputs. Lender owns credit. SOP 50 10 8.1 is the constraint, not a badge |

**GTM / vertical**
- CPA/bookkeeper monthly close is the channel they are not running. Eilla sells a founder demo. Bizbe sells SEO + BizBuySell. You sell through the person who already touches the books. P&C annual review is the challenger, not a second homepage.
- Stay in HVAC/plumbing/electrical in two counsel-cleared states. Do not chase Eilla’s agency/SaaS auction or Bizbe’s FedEx routes. Their home-services language is unearned.
- Listed-owner teasers only with a real package or a real buyer. Never “AI broker” against McKinney’s directory listing.

**Service delivery**
- Publish the split: agents classify, extract, link, draft; humans adjudicate add-backs, run the owner interview, verify the fix, talk to reviewers. Eilla hides this behind “senior advisors.” Bizbe hides it behind “team and platform.”
- Speed metric is fully loaded hours per verified-ready owner, not 15-day outreach-to-offer. Certainty metric is supported vs excluded earnings plus a completed drill, not “under contract” on a listing card.
- Use Bizbe’s fee mess as a wedge: one page, one price, what is included, what is not verified, who is the licensed party. Then beat them on verification, not on commission rate.
- Economics: $1,500 now for current value. Take-rate is an earned option after non-exclusive activation, not LTV in the pitch.

## 5. Threat assessment and 90-day moves

**Ranked risks**
1. **Category collapse** — you are read as another AI broker. Eilla wins pedigree. Bizbe wins UX and listings. You have neither public close nor live inventory. *Counter:* lead with Continuity Evidence / keep-sellable. Never use their category words on the first screen.
2. **Continuity Evidence fails the 28 Aug / 18 Sep gates** — if owners will not pay or connect books pre-trigger, the unique wedge is gone. *Counter:* honor the written kills. Redirect to TransferOS inside broker/lender workflows. Do not improvise an AI-broker pivot.
3. **Eilla walks down-market into US home services** — they already have an under-€5M door. Banker process + PR would look like a grown-up Scorta to investors. *Counter:* be deeper in one trade and one lender export. They cannot run a continuity drill from London content.
4. **Bizbe expands home services off the ISP beachhead** — same owner UX, new vertical. They will get there with listings before you get there with rails if you staff a marketplace. *Counter:* race verified remediations and CPA repeat-sends, not listings.
5. **Proof vacuum** — CreateX is a real logo. May $35k and two unsigned-close mandates will not survive a side-by-side. *Counter:* scoreboard is VROs, not CIMs. One lender-accepted export beats ten teasers.

**Four highest-leverage moves in the next 3–6 months**
1. Earn 20 verified-ready owners in HVAC/plumbing/electrical via CPA/P&C — the product neither sells.
2. Freeze one lender export; get three unaffiliated reviewers to use it.
3. Kill AI-broker language; publish price, provenance, and non-exclusivity.
4. Reuse DealIQ on seller books; do not staff CIM, listings, or Close Desk.

**90-day sentence if this works:** we connected N field-service businesses, source-proved their earnings, removed a real continuity blocker, and a lender’s missing-item list got shorter. Closings can still be lumpy. That is the start of transaction rails. It is not a faster Bizbe and it is not a cheaper Eilla.
