# The Sub-$10M Broker Operating System

## 1. Executive Summary

This report treats the independent broker as a transaction operator, not a listing publisher. The core job is to convert a seller's messy operating evidence into a credible earnings bridge, a controlled buyer process, a lender-ready file, and a closing that does not lose control of confidentiality or funds. The evidence also shows why the work remains human-heavy: the calendar is long, the facts are heterogeneous, and the highest-value decisions concern credibility, disclosure timing, buyer fit, and negotiation rather than document assembly.

- **Process Duration**: A lower-middle-market sale commonly runs **6-12 months** from signed engagement to funds flow; preparation and materials typically consume **6-10 weeks**, marketing and NDA administration another **6-10 weeks**, IOIs **4-8 weeks**, LOIs **2-4 weeks**, and exclusivity, confirmatory diligence, and documents **8-12 weeks**. These are practitioner ranges, not service-level guarantees. [22] [22] [22] [22] [22] [22] -> CASE should make every dependency, owner, and deadline visible while parallel workstreams run.
- **Funnel Reality**: A practitioner illustration starts with **150 qualified contacts**, produces about **40 NDAs/CIM recipients**, **12 IOIs**, **5 management meetings**, **2-3 LOIs**, and **1 close**. It is not a market-wide close rate, but it identifies the conversion data Scorta must record by source, buyer type, price band, and failure reason. [22] -> Build a measurable funnel, not an AI claim about guaranteed conversion.
- **Buyer Mix**: IBBA's Q4 2025 survey covered **350** brokers and advisors; **72%** expected conditions to be at or above the 2021 peak, **54%** expected near-term volume growth, and **71%** expected multiples to hold steady. First-time buyers represented **46%** of Main Street acquisitions, individuals represented **44%** of LMM acquisitions, and private equity represented about one fifth of LMM acquisitions. [13] [13] [13] [13] -> Buyer qualification and SBA readiness matter as much as buyer volume.
- **Financing Gate**: SBA 7(a) has a stated maximum loan amount of **$5M** and a guarantee of **85%** for loans of $150,000 or less and **75%** above $150,000. Form 1919 is submitted by the applicant to a participating lender and supplies eligibility information; Form 413 supports the lender's assessment of financial situation and creditworthiness. [38] [29] [28] -> The SBA lender flow should be a structured evidence package with lender review, not an automated approval engine.
- **October Catalyst**: Official SBA materials state that SOP 50 10 8.1 becomes effective **October 1, 2026**, applies to applications issued an SBA loan number on or after that date, and requires continued use of 8.0 for applications submitted through September 30, 2026. The notice also says change-of-ownership updates are in Appendix 15 and that forms are being updated. [2] Scorta's ground truth makes a QoE requirement for deals at or above **$3M** a hard product gate; the public notice confirms the date but does not itself spell out the full QoE mechanics. -> Version the lender flow by effective rule set and never silently mix 8.0 and 8.1.
- **Tooling Reality**: Public evidence supports the existence and feature sets of marketplaces, valuation packages, VDRs, e-signature tools, and API connections, but not a reliable broker-wide adoption ranking. BizBuySell says it is a marketing platform that charges advertising fees rather than a brokerage; its six-month listing prices shown publicly are **$65.95**, **$89.95**, and **$199.95** per month for three plans. [7] [7] -> Treat Excel, email, generic CRM, PDF, and manual exports as the compatibility baseline until Scorta measures its own broker population.
- **Control Point**: Staged disclosure is a real operating discipline: teaser, NDA, CIM, limited data after IOI, and full room only in exclusivity. [22] VDR products advertise file-level permissions, watermarking, and engagement analytics, but those are brochure claims, not proof that small brokers use them consistently. [18] [10] -> Make identity, consent, purpose, and access logging first-class data, not a final compliance layer.
- **Legal Boundary**: The federal M&A broker exemption contains strict limits, including no custody of transaction funds or securities, no binding authority, no public offering, no financing by the broker, and no passive buyer transaction. [5] [5] State real-estate statutes and any stock-sale, lending, escrow, tax, or insurance activity require separate analysis. -> CASE must block prohibited actions and route jurisdictional uncertainty to licensed counsel.
- **Highest-Leverage Build**: The best near-term automation is not autonomous valuation or autonomous negotiation. It is a source-controlled earnings bridge, completeness and exception detection, staged VDR administration, buyer and lender packet assembly, Q&A routing, and a closing control tower. These functions reduce rework while preserving human judgment and Scorta's ground truth: procedures and findings reconciled to filed returns, no unreported cash, no reliance letters, two-tier identified versus statistical storage, and no three-party fee conflict.

The strategic choice is therefore verification-first orchestration. Scorta can be faster than a spreadsheet-and-email broker without pretending that a software score is a valuation, a QoE conclusion, a credit decision, or legal advice.

## 2. Detailed End-to-End Process Playbook

### Operating sequence and timing

The sale has one critical path and several parallel lanes. The critical path is normally seller authority -> defensible financial story -> buyer selection -> LOI -> confirmatory diligence -> financing and closing. Marketing, lender pre-screening, tax and legal issue spotting, and data-room seeding should begin before an LOI where possible. The most useful public practitioner description places preparation, outreach, bidding, exclusivity, and closing on the calendar ranges below. [22] [22] [22] [22]

The process map has two practical implications. First, the broker's real unit of work is an evidence-linked issue, not a file upload. Second, a lender-ready deal is not simply a deal with more documents: it is a deal where the source, calculation, owner, exception, and next decision are all explicit. That is the operating design target for CASE and Boardroom.

The recurring extraction pattern is a hybrid, not a clean API pipeline: OAuth where available, seller-authorized exports where not, CPA assistance for tax and desktop data, and manual exception handling for leases, add-backs, owner expenses, cash, inventory, and related parties. A robust ingestion layer therefore needs four modes: read-only connector, seller-uploaded export, accountant-mediated upload, and structured manual entry with evidence attachment.

A useful source record contains entity, system, account or report name, period, accounting basis, currency, timezone, extraction method, extraction timestamp, preparer, permissions, raw-file hash, transformation version, and reviewer. This makes a later findings statement reproducible. It also prevents a model from treating a single bank feed or POS report as the authoritative financial statement.

## 5. Integration and Connection Opportunities

### 5.1 Read-only ingestion with purpose control

Scorta should start with seller-authorized OAuth and least-privilege scopes. The consent screen should say which entity, systems, periods, categories, and purpose are covered; separate accounting, banking, payroll, and customer-data consent; and provide revocation. Xero's OAuth model uses specific scopes and access tokens. [11] Square's model similarly requests specific seller permissions and exchanges an authorization code for an access token. [8] Plaid's public description reinforces account selection, data minimization, enforcement of approved data types, and revocation. [32]

The product should not request or retain a bank password, should not make a new use of an IRS transcript because it is technically available, and should not let a model train on identified deal data. The engagement letter and source consent should define purpose, users, retention, permitted derived fields, and deletion. The two-tier design is mandatory: identified source records and deal work product stay in a segregated vault; only explicitly approved statistical aggregates can leave it, with suppression and re-identification controls. A transcript or tax-return artifact is purpose-locked to the engagement and permitted workflow.

### 5.2 File to Findings Report to familiar lender packet

The File should be a source ledger and evidence graph, not a narrative conclusion. Each normalized line should point to the source report, period, calculation, adjustment reason, seller or CPA support, reviewer, and unresolved exception. The Findings Report should then state:

1. The procedures performed.
2. The documents and periods examined.
3. The reconciliation of books, bank or processor data, and filed returns.
4. The exceptions, limitations, missing evidence, and management explanations.
5. The resulting factual schedules and bridges.
6. The intended user and purpose.
7. An explicit statement that it is not an audit, valuation conclusion, credit approval, reliance letter, or guarantee.

That format fits the existing broker and lender habit of receiving a cover memo, historical financials, recast schedule, tax returns, bank statements, debt schedule, projections, purchase agreement, and exception list. It does not ask the lender to accept a novel score in place of underwriting. Form 1919 establishes the borrower-to-lender eligibility information channel, while Form 413 covers applicant financial situation and creditworthiness. [29] [28]

### 5.3 Syndication without losing the VDR

The canonical listing object should contain a listing ID, anonymous teaser, approved ranges, geography policy, industry tags, disclosure tier, permitted syndication channels, expiry date, and a canonical Scorta inquiry endpoint. Marketplace exports should contain no customer names, exact address, employee names, bank details, or source-file links. BizBuySell explicitly supports blind listings by limiting location and generalizing details. [7] It also says it is a marketing platform rather than a brokerage. [7]

A buyer who arrives through BizBuySell, Axial, a franchise referral, or a direct email should receive the same identity and NDA gate. The Scorta VDR remains the system of record; syndication channels receive a teaser and status callbacks, not a copied diligence room. Axial's seller-control language supports this operating pattern: the platform says sellers control whom, how, and when information is shared. [14]

### 5.4 Buyer network and reciprocal referrals

Buyer sharing should be based on buyer identity, mandate, reputation, sector, geography, financing capacity, and prior transaction behavior, not on indiscriminate list exchange. Create a network record with source, consent, data fields permitted to share, expiry, conflicts, referral terms, and whether the buyer is acting as principal, agent, lender, or service provider. The recipient sees a redacted opportunity card until the seller authorizes outreach; the buyer must sign the relevant NDA before name-level disclosure.

Commercially, the network must honor Scorta's rules: no three-party charge on the same deal, no buyer fee for the diligence packet on Scorta's own listings, and verification revenue primarily on third-party deals. A referral fee or cooperating-broker split must be disclosed, authorized, and recorded. A network recommendation cannot create undisclosed dual representation, an implied agency relationship, or a fee payable by a party that was told the service was free.

### 5.5 Lender packet and SBA rule versioning

Create a lender-neutral packet with stable filenames and a lender-specific cover sheet. The minimum index should include:

| Packet block | Contents and control |
|---|---|
| Borrower and transaction | Applicant and owners, entity records, purchase agreement or LOI, acquisition entity, purchase price, allocation, sources and uses, and requested loan purpose. |
| Historical evidence | Filed returns, interim statements, GL or trial balance, bank and merchant statements, payroll, debt schedule, recast bridge, and Findings Report. |
| Buyer and guarantor | Form 1919, Form 413, resumes, experience, personal financial information, credit authorization, equity source, and contingent liabilities as requested by the lender. |
| Repayment case | Historical and projected cash flow, DSCR model with visible assumptions, working-capital needs, owner compensation, transition plan, and sensitivity cases. |
| Collateral and transfer | Lease assignment, franchise approval, licenses, insurance, appraisal, environmental work where applicable, UCC or lien evidence, payoff letters, and seller-note terms. |
| Closing and compliance | Form 159 if applicable, fee and compensation disclosure, final conditions, wire controls, escrow instructions, signed authority, and funds-flow memo. |

The official SBA page states the $5M 7(a) maximum and guarantee percentages, but those facts do not replace lender-specific underwriting. [38] The official notice says 8.1 applications are keyed to the date the SBA loan number is issued and that updated forms may lag the SOP. [2] Scorta should store `sba_rule_version`, `loan_number_date`, `form_version`, `lender`, and `condition_owner` on every package. The product must treat Scorta's stated October 1 QoE gate for deals at or above $3M as a hard workflow rule, while labeling the detailed interpretation as lender and counsel controlled until the operative Appendix 15 text is fully implemented.

### 6.1 Licensing and representation boundaries

The design consequence is a licensing matrix keyed to activity, not only to company location. Each engagement should answer: Is real estate being sold or leased? Is a security being transferred? Does Scorta handle or direct transaction funds? Is it providing or arranging financing? Is a buyer passive? Is a referral fee paid? Is an out-of-state broker involved? Is the employee or contractor licensed where required? The CASE kernel should stop the workflow when an answer is unknown.

### 6.2 Engagement letter and conflict controls

An engagement letter should identify the seller, entity, authority, scope, territory, exclusivity, start and end date, termination rights, tail period, fee trigger, fee base, retainer or minimum if any, expense approval, cooperating-broker treatment, referral compensation, and payment timing. It should distinguish an asset sale, equity sale, merger, real-estate component, and financing support. It should disclose whether the firm represents only the seller, whether it may communicate with buyers, and how any dual-representation or transaction-broker issue will be handled.

The data provisions should define source systems, transcript and tax-return purpose, permitted users, identified versus statistical storage, retention, deletion, subprocessors, breach notification, seller review, and the fact that a model output is not a reliance letter. The seller should represent authority to provide records and agree that false, incomplete, or unreported information can end the engagement. Scorta's refusal of unreported cash belongs in the engagement and the decline protocol, not only in an internal policy.

### 6.3 Risk flags and the broker response

- **Unreported cash or off-book activity**: refuse to market it or convert it into an add-back. Stop, document the issue, and require lawful correction and filed-return support. Do not build a valuation or lender package on a number the seller will not stand behind.
- **Owner dependence**: identify decisions, customer relationships, sales, estimating, technical work, passwords, and licenses controlled by the owner. Management meetings test whether a second layer can operate the business. [22] Build a transition and retention plan or discount and structure the risk.
- **Customer or vendor concentration**: quantify by customer, contract, geography, and relationship owner; obtain consent and renewal evidence; avoid naming customers before the right disclosure tier. Public practitioner guidance emphasizes margin by customer, region, segment, and service. [17]
- **Lease assignment and key-person risk**: put landlord, franchisor, licensor, and key employee consent on the critical path. A buyer cannot close a business whose premises, franchise, permit, or essential employee cannot transfer.
- **Working capital and inventory**: define normal working capital before the LOI, use the same accounts in the peg and closing statement, and preserve the inventory count and valuation date. A simple working-capital calculation is AR plus inventory less AP and accrued expenses. [17]
- **Tax and SALT exposure**: obtain returns, notices, payroll and sales-tax filings, and voluntary-disclosure status. SALT liabilities can pause a deal, reduce price, or produce holdbacks. [17]
- **Cybersecurity and insurance**: obtain a threat assessment and insurance schedule where material. Wipfli identifies cybersecurity as relevant to insurers, financial institutions, and buyers. [17]
- **Seller financing and earnouts**: model collection risk, subordination, standby, security, payment source, and default remedies; never treat contingent value as cash at close. If an SBA lender or counsel imposes a form or prohibition, the lender's rule controls. A seller note should be a visible source and use, not a hidden bridge.

### 6.4 Bank deal-killers and pre-emption

There is no reliable public dataset giving a lender decline rate by cause for this market. The practical pre-screen should nevertheless test repayment capacity, buyer experience, credit and liquidity, equity source, historical tax consistency, supported add-backs, lease and franchise transfer, collateral or lien issues, environmental exposure, customer concentration, owner replacement, payroll and tax compliance, and the seller-note or earnout structure. Form 1919 is required to determine eligibility information, and Form 413 is used to assess financial situation and creditworthiness. [29] [28]

Pre-emption means making every lender concern visible before the LOI or at least before exclusivity: a tax-to-book bridge, a buyer experience memo, an equity-source file, a lease consent plan, a repayment model, a transition budget, and a list of unsupported adjustments. The lender should receive a concise cover memo that points to evidence, not a black-box Scorta Score. The score can prioritize review, but it must not represent lender approval.

### 6.5 Fees, insurance, time, and failed deals

Market fee forms include contingent success fees, retainers, minimum fees, flat fees, and modified Lehman-style schedules, but the reviewed public material did not provide an IBBA fee census or a reliable sub-$10M adoption percentage. Therefore this report does not publish a supposedly typical percentage. The engagement should state whether the success fee is based on enterprise value, equity value, cash and non-cash consideration, seller note, earnout, rollover, assumed debt, working-capital adjustment, or only cash received. It should state when the fee is earned and how later consideration is treated.

A cooperating-broker split must be agreed before introduction, with party, scope, amount, invoice path, and disclosure. Scorta must not create a three-sided fee arrangement. On Scorta-owned listings, the buyer receives the diligence packet at no charge; verification revenue is primarily from third-party deals. That fact should be encoded in the commercial ledger and buyer disclosures.

Small firms should carry professional E&O appropriate to the activity, cyber coverage for identified financial data, crime or wire-fraud controls, and written subcontractor and referral protections. Public evidence did not support a typical limit or premium benchmark. Likewise, the **6-12 month** calendar is supported, but hours per engagement, internal cost, seller time, and post-close support cost are not. FIH describes running the company while selling it as a second full-time job involving data requests, meetings, advisor calls, and diligence sessions. [22] Scorta should create its own benchmark by logging active work minutes, elapsed days, rework, third-party cost, and failure reason by stage.

A failed listing should be re-engaged only after the failure has been classified: price, evidence, buyer universe, confidentiality leak, financing, seller behavior, diligence, or legal/transferability. Change the defective variable, refresh all dated data, obtain new consent, and preserve the original process history. Relaunching an unchanged CIM is not recovery.

## Synthesis

Four operating models compete in this market, and they optimize different dimensions:

| Model | Primary mechanism | Scope and evidence | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public marketplace | Broad listing reach, anonymous or semi-anonymous inquiries, advertising, and broker directory discovery. | BizBuySell explicitly describes marketing rather than brokerage and supports blind listings. [7] | Reach is high, but qualification, source reconciliation, staged diligence, and lender readiness remain with the broker. |
| Confidential LMM network | Buyer research, targeted outreach, controlled disclosure, and transaction workflow. | Axial advertises buyer criteria, reputation data, seller control, and lower-middle-market reach. [14] | Better control and buyer fit, but platform access and actual conversion economics are not publicly benchmarked. |
| Independent or franchise broker | Relationship-led origination, spreadsheet recast, local buyer knowledge, direct negotiation, and manual coordination. | IBBA's survey demonstrates an active intermediary market and diverse buyer mix, while VR publicly describes a bridge between brokerage and investment banking. [13] [9] | Flexible and trusted, but key-person dependence, inconsistent evidence, and fragmented tools create rework. |
| Scorta verification-led firm | End-to-end sell-side execution plus source-controlled File, Findings Report, VDR, Boardroom, lender flow, and buyer network. | Ground truth sets the constraints: no unreported cash, no reliance conclusions, no three-party fees, free buyer packet on Scorta-owned listings, third-party verification revenue, and two-tier data. | Requires disciplined consent, legal routing, and human review. It should compete on evidence and process reliability, not on replacing buyer, lender, counsel, CPA, or broker judgment. |

The non-obvious tension is speed versus disclosure quality. A fast teaser with weak financial support creates more noise, more NDAs, and a weaker lender file. A slower preparation stage can produce a cleaner buyer set and better cash certainty, but only if the seller remains engaged and the business continues performing. The practitioner sequence of teaser -> NDA -> CIM -> limited post-IOI data -> full exclusivity room is therefore not bureaucracy; it is a risk-allocation mechanism. [22]

The second tension is standardization versus reality. APIs can retrieve transactions, but a POS settlement, a tax return, a bank deposit, and an owner add-back answer different questions. A standardized evidence schema should preserve those differences rather than flatten them. The File and Findings Report are valuable precisely because they expose limitations and exceptions instead of making heterogeneous systems look uniform.

The third tension is lender compatibility versus product differentiation. SBA Forms 1919 and 413, lender checklists, tax returns, recast schedules, and signed purchase documents are familiar interfaces. Scorta should improve their lineage and completeness, not force a credit officer to accept an opaque score. The October 2026 SOP change makes this more urgent: versioned QoE and lender packaging can become a defensible wedge if the firm keeps official requirements, lender interpretation, and Scorta operating policy visibly separate.

The decision is to build the evidence graph and control plane first. CASE governs permission and risk. File preserves sources. Findings Report states procedures and findings. VDR governs disclosure. Boardroom drives dependencies and closing. Scorta Score prioritizes readiness with transparent missingness. SBA lender flow exports familiar evidence. Buyer network improves fit without creating confidentiality or fee conflicts. That architecture collapses the highest-friction work while keeping the human broker accountable for judgment and the lender, CPA, attorney, appraiser, and specialist accountable for their own decisions.
